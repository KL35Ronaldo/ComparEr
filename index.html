  1. Open Instagram and tap your Profile. Tap the menu icon (☰) at the top right.
  2. Go to Accounts Center (sometimes under Settings & Privacy).
  3. Select Your information and permissions, then Download your information.
  4. Choose your Instagram profile from the list.
  5. Tap Select types of information, then choose Followers and following.
  6. Choose JSON (preferred) or HTML as the file format, and set the date range to All time.
  7. Tap Create files. Instagram will notify you (usually within a few minutes to a few hours) when the export is ready.
  8. Download the export ZIP from the notification or from the same Download your information screen.
  9. Upload the downloaded .zip file here — do not extract it first.
